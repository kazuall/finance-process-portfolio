Holdings and freshness monitoring

PythonSQLiteReactLinux VPS (systemd)Read-only sources (IBKR, OKX, Kraken, Solana, Lighter)

My broker, exchanges and wallets update at different times. To value a position, I need to know when the account was read and how recent its price is.

Architecture

Integrity checks

Holdings stores each observation by source, account and instrument. A missing price leaves the valuation unavailable. Net account value and leveraged exposure have separate fields. The monitoring pilot tracks balance and price timestamps independently and requires a successful, fresh reading before closing an incident.

Verified result

Holdings runs as a systemd service on a Linux VPS and collects read-only data from five live platforms. Backups are encrypted, and I verified an isolated restore.

In a separate synthetic pilot test, the balance was 601 seconds old and its price was 10 seconds old. Only the balance exceeded its threshold (180 seconds for balances, 300 for prices). Repeated checks produced one incident report for that episode.

Bank sync and invoice review

Actual BudgetEnable Banking APIBank reconciliationSHA-256

I connected my two bank accounts to Actual Budget through Enable Banking. I now start a sync in Actual instead of downloading statements. When I switched from CSV imports, I checked whether the bank feed matched transactions already in my budget.

Architecture

Bank sync

Invoice intake

Integrity checks

I compared budget snapshots before and after the first sync on one account, then checked the booked balance against the bank, treating pending items separately. Invoice review follows its own workflow. A SHA-256 fingerprint verifies the original file, and visual review caught an incorrect amount on a cancelled invoice.

Verified result

On the first sync of that account, 235 existing entries gained bank references and 7 new transactions appeared. The comparison found no duplicates or deletions. After I entered the opening balance, the booked balance matched the bank to the cent.

Calendar changes and application replies

n8nPythonSQLiteLocal LLM (NuExtract)PII maskingGoogle Calendar API

A rescheduled invitation can create a second appointment. A reply from an employer can concern just one of two open applications. I use local AI extraction to propose changes and source checks to decide which record can be updated.

Architecture

Calendar updates

Application replies

Integrity checks

The calendar workflow checks dates, time zone and, for invitation updates, event ID and version. It reads each write back by event ID. Eligible calendar excerpts are masked before external classification, and attachments stay local. The tracker checks that the quote appears in the current message and matches the employer and role. Conflicting evidence goes to human review.

Verified result

A rescheduled interview produced 1 event at the revised time, with 0 actions left on replay. The calendar fix passed 83 tests. In a separate tracker case, a rejection changed only the relevant one of two applications at the same employer.
